<section>
  <p>
    I want a place to keep working notes that isn't a private notebook. Most of what
    I write down each week is for me: half-formed ideas, paper margins, things I want
    to come back to once I've understood them better. A small fraction is worth
    sharing — and the act of writing for someone else, even hypothetically, tends to
    force the loose ends into something coherent.
  </p>
  <p>
    So this page exists for two reasons. The selfish one: writing publicly is a
    forcing function. The less selfish one: I think a lot of mathematics is more
    welcoming when you can see other people working on it, rather than only the
    finished version of the result. If you're a student wandering in here, I hope it
    looks like a desk in progress.
  </p>
</section>

<section>
  <h3 class="section-label">What I expect to put here</h3>
  <p>
    A rough taxonomy, subject to change as I figure out what's actually useful:
  </p>
  <ul>
    <li>
      <strong>Reading notes.</strong> Short summaries and reactions as I work through
      papers — what the result is, what I find clear, what I still need to chase down.
    </li>
    <li>
      <strong>Half-formed ideas.</strong> Questions I want to come back to,
      techniques I'd like to understand better, conjectures I'm not yet sure about.
    </li>
    <li>
      <strong>Teaching scribbles.</strong> Worked examples, explanations I've
      settled on after several attempts in office hours, notes for future me.
    </li>
    <li>
      <strong>The occasional essay.</strong> When something is worth more than a
      few paragraphs — on mathematics, on learning, on a paper that changed my
      view of something.
    </li>
  </ul>
</section>

<section>
  <h3 class="section-label">How often</h3>
  <p>
    Irregularly. I'd rather post something I'm willing to put my name to than fill
    a schedule. If a stretch of silence here means I'm deep in a paper, that's the
    point.
  </p>
</section>
