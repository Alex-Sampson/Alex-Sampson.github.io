Suggested Classroom Rules

  1. Use 2–6 balanced teams (roughly 3–5 students each). Spread students with previous calculus experience across the teams when possible.
  2. Every team writes a response. When time is called, teams reveal simultaneously; every correct team earns the displayed value.
  3. The team marked NEXT PICK chooses the clue. The pick rotates automatically after each revealed clue, regardless of the scores.
  4. Use half value for a substantially correct idea with incomplete justification. Do not deduct points on ordinary clues.
  5. Rotate each team’s spokesperson after four clues; do not repeat a spokesperson until everyone on that team has had a turn.
  6. On a Daily Double, the chooser wagers before seeing the clue. Other teams still answer and may earn the clue’s printed value. Final Jeopardy wagers should be written on paper.
  7. The Practice clue is unscored. For Sudden Death, only tied teams respond: correct teams survive, incorrect teams are eliminated, and an all-correct or all-incorrect result sends everyone to the next tie-breaker.
  8. Collect one work sheet per team for attendance.

Suggested timing: 5 minutes for setup, 5 for a practice clue, 45–50 for the board, 10 for Final Jeopardy, and 5 for debrief.
