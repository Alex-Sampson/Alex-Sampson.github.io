Research interests

  • Shape analysis & infinite-dimensional geometry: Riemannian geometry of shape spaces; geometric PDEs on spaces of curves and surfaces.
  • Optimal transport & Wasserstein geometry: Analytical and geometric frameworks for shapes, signals, and distributions.
  • PDEs in applications: Qualitative properties of solutions to models from physics and biology.
  • Variational & topological methods: Weak solution frameworks, energy methods, and topological degree theory.
