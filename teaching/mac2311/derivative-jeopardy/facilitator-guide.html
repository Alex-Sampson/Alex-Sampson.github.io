MAC2311 Derivative Jeopardy

Facilitator guide · Recitation 2 · Coverage through §4.1.3

Run of Show

0–5 minUse 4–6 teams (roughly 3–5 students each); distribute scratch/work sheets; assign recorder and spokesperson. 5–10 minExplain rules and run an unscored practice clue. 10–58 minMain board. Aim for 90 seconds per standard clue and 2 minutes for computations. 58–68 minFinal Jeopardy. Teams write wagers before the clue appears. 68–75 minDebrief two frequently missed ideas and collect one work sheet per team.

Suggested Rules

  1. Build balanced teams when possible, spreading students with previous calculus experience across the room.
  2. Every team writes a response and reveals it simultaneously when time is called. Every correct team earns full value.
  3. Require the answer form stated on each problem. After revealing the solution, enter all exact team totals and click Submit All Scores once.
  4. The team marked NEXT PICK chooses the clue. The pick rotates automatically after every revealed clue, regardless of correctness.
  5. Rotate spokespersons every four clues, with no repeats until everyone has spoken. Work sheets provide the attendance record.
  6. Every wager screen displays the current scoreboard so teams can evaluate the risk before wagering.
  7. On a Daily Double, the NEXT PICK team chooses one wager before the problem appears. The same wager applies to every team.
  8. Final Jeopardy opens with a wager screen for every team. Every trailing team may wager enough to finish $50 above the current leader.

Practice Clue

Unscored · 60 seconds

Suppose f is decreasing immediately to the left of x=c and increasing immediately to the right. Classify the local extremum at x=c. Answer format: local maximum, local minimum, or neither.
Worked solution:
  1. Decreasing to the left gives f′<0.
  2. Increasing to the right gives f′>0.
  3. The change from negative to positive gives a local minimum by the First Derivative Test.
Conclusion: The function has a local minimum at x=c. Use this problem to demonstrate the timer, reveal, scoring, and return-to-board controls; it does not rotate NEXT PICK.

Sudden Death Bank

Only tied teams respond. Correct teams survive and incorrect teams are eliminated. If all remaining teams are correct or all are incorrect, nobody is eliminated; use the next question. These questions are unscored and allow 45 seconds each.

  1. True or false: If f′(c)=0, then f must have a local extremum at x=c. Answer: False.
  2. If f′<0 and f″<0 on an interval, describe the graph. Answer: Decreasing and concave down.
  3. c is a critical number and f″(c)>0. What is the verdict? Answer: A local minimum at x=c.
  4. Can c be a critical number when f′(c) does not exist? Answer: Yes, provided f(c) exists.
  5. f is continuous at c, and f″ changes from positive to negative there. What happens? Answer: An inflection point; concavity changes from up to down.
  6. f′(c)=0, but f′ is positive immediately before and after c. Classify the point. Answer: Neither a local maximum nor a local minimum.
