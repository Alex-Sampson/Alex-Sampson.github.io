Suggested Classroom Rules

  1. Use 2–6 balanced teams (roughly 3–5 students each). Spread students with previous calculus experience across the teams when possible.
  2. Every team writes a response. When time is called, teams reveal simultaneously; every correct team earns the displayed value.
  3. The team marked NEXT PICK chooses the clue. The pick rotates automatically after each revealed clue, regardless of the scores.
  4. After revealing the worked solution, enter all new team totals and click Submit All Scores once.
  5. Rotate each team’s spokesperson after four clues; do not repeat a spokesperson until everyone on that team has had a turn.
  6. Every wager screen displays the current scoreboard so teams can evaluate the risk before wagering.
  7. On a Daily Double, the NEXT PICK team chooses one wager before seeing the problem. That wager applies equally to every team.
  8. Final Jeopardy begins with a wager screen for every team. Every trailing team may wager enough to finish $50 above the current leader.
  9. The Practice clue is unscored. For Sudden Death, only tied teams respond: correct teams survive, incorrect teams are eliminated, and an all-correct or all-incorrect result sends everyone to the next tie-breaker.
  10. Collect one work sheet per team for attendance.

Suggested timing: 5 minutes for setup, 5 for a practice clue, 45–50 for the board, 10 for Final Jeopardy, and 5 for debrief.
